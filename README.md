# The Nine Realms

*Every journey begins somewhere. Mine begins here.*

There are nine realms to this path. Each one opens a door to a deeper understanding of the world of computing.

I have only just stepped onto the path. There is much I do not know, much I have yet to build, and even more I have yet to understand.

For now, I am a cultivator of the First Realm.

## A Few Rules of the Path

- I will learn the fundamentals, not just the tools built upon them.
- I will build things, break things, and understand why they work.
- I will not mistake familiarity for mastery.
- I will use AI to improve my work, not to replace my understanding.
- I will document what I learn and leave evidence of the journey.

## The Journey

**Current Realm:** One

**Realms Conquered:** None. The journey has only begun.

**Destination:** To become a computer scientist and engineer capable of understanding existing technologies and creating new ones.
